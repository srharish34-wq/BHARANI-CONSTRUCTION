Who we are

Bharani Engineers & Contractors was set up to offer professional civil construction and design services. An experienced management team and a seasoned technical workforce deliver design, contracting and construction management as each client requires.

We take up challenging infrastructure and building projects: factories, commercial complexes, hotels, office buildings and independent villas. We also specialise in post-tensioning for buildings and civil structures, and in large structural steel fabrication for the growing infrastructure industry.

  • Design, contracting and construction management
  • Post-tensioned slab systems for buildings
  • Structural steel and PEB solutions

Our journey

From a small civil contractor to an industrial infra developer.

  1. 2011

    Founded in Chennai

    Started as a civil contracting firm serving local industrial units.

  2. 2014

    First PEB project

    Entered pre-engineered buildings with our first factory shed.

  3. 2017

    Flooring and roofing

    Added trimix, epoxy flooring and PPGI / PUF roofing crews.

  4. 2020

    Utilities and treatment plants

    Took up ETP, STP, underground tanks and fire hydrant works.

  5. 2026

    200+ projects delivered

    Repeat work for automobile, pharma, cargo and bearings plants.
