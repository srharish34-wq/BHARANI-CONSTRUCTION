About Bharani Engineers & Contractors

We offer professional design, contracting and construction management, led by an experienced management team and a seasoned technical workforce.

Our projects span factories, warehouses, commercial complexes, hotels, canteens, and administrative buildings, including ETP/STP projects, fire hydrant systems, and underground (UG) tanks, delivered with a strict focus on site safety and timely completion.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which areas do you serve?

    We work across Chennai and India, and take up industrial sites in neighbouring states on request.

    How long does a PEB building take?

    Most buildings are completed in 8–16 weeks depending on size, after drawings and foundation are ready.

    Do you handle design and approvals?

    Yes. We provide design, drawings and construction management as per your requirement.

    Can I get a quotation before the site visit?

    Use the calculator or the enquiry form for an indicative figure. A site visit confirms the final quote.

    Do you offer maintenance after handover?

    Yes. Roofing, flooring and painting maintenance contracts are available.
