01

Floor Coating

Floors that carry forklifts, machines and daily traffic without cracking.

  • Base preparation and levels checked before every pour or coating
  • Joint planning and curing to prevent cracks
  • Clear handover with care and maintenance advice
6 types

Types of flooring we deliver

01

Trimix flooring

Heavy-duty vacuum-dewatered concrete floors with a hardened, level surface. Built for forklifts, racking and heavy machine loads. Joints are planned to limit cracking.

02

Epoxy flooring

Seamless, chemical-resistant coating over a prepared concrete base. Easy to clean and suited to production, packing and clean areas. Available in slip-resistant finishes.

03

PU flooring

Flexible polyurethane floors that handle thermal shock and frequent washing. A good fit for food, pharma and cold areas. Cures to a hygienic, joint-free surface.

04

Paver block flooring

Interlocking concrete blocks laid over a compacted base. Ideal for yards, parking and internal roads. Individual blocks can be lifted and replaced easily.

05

Polished concrete flooring

Concrete ground and polished to a smooth, glossy finish. Low maintenance and dust-free for showrooms and assembly bays. Needs no extra coating or tiles.

06

Industrial tiles

Anti-skid, heavy-duty tiles fixed over a levelled base. Suited to plants, canteens and wash areas. Grouting is chosen to resist water and light chemicals.

02

Roofing

Weather-tight roofs for sheds, plants and warehouses, in every common system.

  • Sheet profile and thickness matched to your span and wind load
  • Proper flashing, sealing and gutters to prevent leaks
  • Safe working at height with trained crews
6 types

Types of roofing we deliver

01

PPGI roofing

Colour-coated galvanised steel sheets for sheds and wall cladding. Light, quick to install and low on upkeep. Available in profiles and colours to suit your building.

02

PUF panels

Insulated sandwich panels with a polyurethane foam core. Keeps interiors cooler and cuts energy use. Suited to cold rooms, clean rooms and temperature-controlled halls.

03

Polycarbonate sheets

Translucent sheets that let daylight into large halls. Cuts lighting costs during the day. Strong, light and available in several thicknesses.

04

RCC slab

Cast-in-place reinforced concrete roof slabs for permanent buildings. Durable and fire resistant, with waterproofing and slope for drainage planned in.

05

Metal deck

Profiled steel sheets used as permanent formwork for composite floors and roofs. Speeds up construction and reduces shuttering and propping on site.

06

Steel truss

Fabricated steel trusses for long, column-free spans. Designed, welded and erected by our crews, then painted for corrosion protection.

03

PEB buildings

Pre-engineered steel buildings designed, fabricated and erected for speed.

  • Design, fabrication and erection handled by one team
  • Shorter build time than conventional construction
  • Frames sized to your crane, mezzanine and expansion plans
7 types

Types of peb buildings we deliver

01

Clear span

Single-bay frames with no interior columns. Gives open floor space for production lines and storage. Fast to fabricate and erect to your exact width and height.

02

Multi span

Frames with interior columns that cut steel weight on very wide buildings. A cost-effective choice for large warehouses and multi-bay plants.

03

Crane system

Structures with crane beams and brackets designed in from the start. Supports EOT and gantry cranes safely, with column and foundation loads checked for your capacity.

04

Mezzanine floor

An extra working floor built inside your existing building. Uses steel framing with metal deck or RCC. Adds storage or office space without a larger footprint.

05

Office block

Admin and office blocks built on the same PEB frame as the plant. Quick to build, and can be finished with insulated panels, glazing and interiors.

06

Mono slope

Buildings with a single-direction sloping roof. Well suited to sheds, annexes and lean-to extensions attached to existing structures.

07

Gable roof

The standard two-sided sloping roof for general industrial buildings. Sheds rain quickly, and ridge vents or skylights can be added for ventilation and light.

04

Painting

Protective and decorative coatings for plants, steel and roads.

  • Surface preparation before every coat
  • Paint systems chosen for the environment and surface
  • Neat finish with minimal disturbance to operations
6 types

Types of painting we deliver

01

Water paint

Interior and exterior wall finishes after proper surface preparation. Smooth, washable coats in the shades you choose, applied by trained painters.

02

Enamel

Hard-wearing gloss paint for steel, doors, grills and railings. Resists wear and weather, with the right primer chosen for each surface.

03

Fire retardant

Coatings that slow the spread of flame over steel and wood surfaces. Applied to the specified thickness and checked coat by coat.

04

Cement paint

Durable exterior paint for masonry and compound walls. Breathable, weather-resistant and economical for large wall areas.

05

Road marking

Lane lines, parking bays, zebra crossings and safety markings on plant roads. Uses road-grade paint for clear, lasting visibility.

06

Anti-corrosive

Surface cleaning, primer and protective coats that keep steel from rusting. Ideal for PEB structures, tanks, pipes and platforms in harsh environments.

Civil works

  • Work executed to approved drawings and levels
  • Materials tested and concrete cured under supervision
  • One team from excavation to finishing

03

RCC works

05

Plastering

06

Flooring

Base concrete, sub-base and screed prepared for every floor type. Proper levels and curing so the final flooring lasts.

07

Roads

08

Storm water drains
